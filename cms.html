Research Summary

The research showed that content management systems can serve very different purposes. WordPress, Drupal, and Joomla are traditional CMS platforms that can manage both the website content and presentation. Ghost focuses heavily on publishing, memberships, and newsletters, while Strapi takes a headless approach where the CMS manages the content and a separate application is responsible for displaying it.

The best CMS therefore depends on the requirements of the website. A smaller content-based site may benefit from the simplicity and large plugin ecosystem of WordPress, while a more complex organization may benefit from Drupal's structured content and permissions. Headless systems such as Strapi can provide developers more freedom over the front end but also require additional development.
